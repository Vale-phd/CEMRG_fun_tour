Ahh, the theatre! Drink it in, darling.

This stage carries the name of Christopher Marlowe, Canterbury's own, baptised here in fifteen sixty-four, mere weeks before a certain young William Shakespeare. Marlowe gave us mighty verse and a life of glorious scandal, cut tragically short in a tavern brawl. Very dramatic. He would have approved.

Today these boards host plays and players, music and magic, the roar of the crowd, the hush before the curtain. So strike a pose, project from the diaphragm, and remember: this little corner of Canterbury is a stage, and rather a good one.
