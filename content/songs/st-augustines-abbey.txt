CAPTION:
elegiac English folk lament, solo viol, soft harp, distant wordless choir, gentle female alto, slow and mournful, airy reverb, 64 bpm

DURATION: 100

LYRICS:
[Intro]
Grass over the fallen nave

[Verse 1]
Here a monastery rose,
Where the kings of Kent repose.
Aethelberht and Bertha lie,
Under a wide and quiet sky.

[Chorus]
Sing low for the sleeping stones,
For buried kings and their hollow thrones.
A thousand years of bell and prayer,
Now only wind in the open air.

[Verse 2]
Monks once copied light to page,
Kept the lamp through a darker age.
Then came the king and the breaking hand,
Down came the towers across the land.

[Outro]
Foundations hold the story still.
