Peace be with you, friend, and welcome. I am a brother of the order of Saint Francis.

We came to England long ago, in the year twelve twenty-four, with nothing but our robes and our trust in God. Here in Canterbury we raised this little chapel, set right upon the river, so the water flows beneath the very stones. It is the oldest Franciscan house in all of England.

We owned nothing, you understand. We begged our bread, we tended the poor, we praised our Maker in the birdsong. Linger a while in the quiet, and listen to the river. There is grace in small and simple things.
