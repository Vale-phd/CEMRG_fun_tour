Oh, marvellous, you found us! Do come in.

This is the Beaney House of Art and Knowledge, and yes, that really is the name, isn't it splendid? Part museum, part gallery, part library, all under one rather handsome old roof. We have paintings and pottery, beetles and butterflies, ancient curiosities and the occasional stuffed surprise.

Mister Beaney left the money to build it, a Canterbury man who made good overseas and sent his fortune home. Generous fellow. Inside it is warm, it is free, and it is gloriously full of things. Wipe your feet, mind the cabinets, and prepare to be delighted.
