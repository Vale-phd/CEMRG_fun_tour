Stand quietly here a moment, if you will.

This is the war memorial, set before the cathedral gate, at the very heart of the city. It carries the names of Canterbury's sons and daughters who went to war and did not come home. Ordinary people: clerks and farmhands, shopkeepers and schoolboys, who left these streets and these gardens and never saw them again.

Each name was a life, a family, a future given up so that others might walk here in peace. We owe them the small kindness of remembering. Pause. Read a name. And then walk gently on.
