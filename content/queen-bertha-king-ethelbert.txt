Hear ye, hear ye! Make way, and lift your eyes to their royal majesties.

Behold King Ethelbert of Kent, and beside him his queen, the lady Bertha. She came from across the sea already a Christian, and she would not give up her faith. So the king gave her a little church to pray in, just up the hill. And when a monk named Augustine arrived from Rome with a message, it was this queen who had already opened the door.

From this very spot, you might say, Christian England began. Bow if you like. They have earned it.
