Right then, hop aboard and mind your step, she's a touch wobbly.

Welcome to the Great Stour, the finest little river in Kent, and I'll hear no argument. Folk have poled boats along here for centuries, hauling cargo, dodging the mill races, having a good moan about the weather. These days we go gentler: a slow drift under the old bridges, past gardens and crooked Tudor walls leaning over the water like they've had one too many.

Keep your hands inside, and watch for the swans, they are bad-tempered devils. And if a low bridge comes along? Duck, or learn to swim. Cast off!
