Pause here, gentle wanderer, where the Great Stour winds soft and silver beneath the ancient wall.

These gardens have charmed the strolling heart for generations: lawns of tender green, beds ablaze with colour, and a great old plane tree, vast and patient, its trunk as wide as a cottage. Willows trail their fingers in the water. Light dances on the ripples. Time, for a moment, forgets to hurry.

The poets came here to dream, and so, perhaps, shall you. Breathe the river air, and let the quiet beauty of Canterbury settle gently on your soul.
