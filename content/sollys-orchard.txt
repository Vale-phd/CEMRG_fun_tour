And now, the showstopper you have all been waiting for: Solly's Orchard. Try to contain your excitement.

It is, and I want to be honest with you, a small green patch by the river. No dramatic ruins. No kings buried here, as far as anyone has bothered to check. But here is the thing about a humble little orchard tucked behind a busy city: it is quiet. It is yours for a minute.

The Stour mutters past, a duck judges you in silence, and the whole mad rush of Canterbury politely waits outside. Honestly? Underrated. Sit down. Do nothing. You are welcome.
