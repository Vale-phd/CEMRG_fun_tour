Halt. State your business, traveller.

These walls? We Romans raised the first of them, near two thousand years ago, when this place was called Durovernum. Good Kentish ragstone, laid by soldiers who would rather have been somewhere warmer. The medieval lot built up on our line, the lazy so-and-sos, but the bones beneath are ours.

We walked this rampart in the rain, watching the road from the coast. Now you stroll it for pleasure. Times change. But the wall still stands, and so, in a manner of speaking, do I. Move along.
